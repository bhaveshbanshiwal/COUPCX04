📜 COUP Rules & Deck Mechanics

1. Setup & Ordered Deck (2–6 Players)

The Court Deck has 15 Cards (3 each of Duke, Assassin, Captain, Ambassador, Contessa). Every player starts with 0 Coins and 2 Cards. Cards drawn come from the TOP, and returned cards go to the BOTTOM.
