📜 COUP Rules & Deck Mechanics

1. Setup & Ordered Deck (2–6 Players)

The Court Deck contains exactly 15 Cards (3× Duke, 3× Assassin, 3× Captain, 3× Ambassador, 3× Contessa). Every player starts with 0 Coins and 2 Face-Down Cards dealt from the top of the deck. The Court Deck order is strictly preserved: cards drawn always come from the TOP, and cards returned always go into the BOTTOM.

2. Actions & Counteractions

Character / Action Action Effect Counteraction (Block)
General — Income Take +1 Coin from the Treasury. Cannot be blocked or challenged.
General — Foreign Aid Take +2 Coins from the Treasury. Blocked by any player claiming Duke.
General — Coup Pay 7 Coins. Target loses 1 card (mandatory at 10+ coins). Unblockable & Unchallengeable.
Duke — Tax Take +3 Coins from the Treasury. Blocks Foreign Aid.
Assassin — Assassinate Pay 3 Coins. Target loses 1 card. Blocked by target claiming Contessa.
Captain — Steal Steal up to 2 Coins from a target player. Blocked by target claiming Captain or Ambassador.
Ambassador — Exchange Draw 2 cards from Top of Court Deck, keep your live count, quietly put 2 cards into Bottom of Court Deck. Blocks Steal.
Contessa — Block Reactive only. Blocks Assassinate.

3. Challenges & Card Visibility

  • When Challenged & You Have the Card (No Bluff): Your card is shown to the table to prove you were not bluffing. That card is then placed at the bottom of the Court Deck, and you draw a replacement card from the top of the Court Deck. The challenger loses 1 card.
  • When Caught Bluffing (Or Losing a Card): Your card is not shown to the other players. You quietly choose 1 of your cards to put into the bottom of the Court Deck, reducing your active card count by 1.
